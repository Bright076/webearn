LOGO SETUP INSTRUCTIONS
========================

To add the WebEarn logo to your site:

1. Save your logo image as: public/logo.png
2. The logo component is already set up at: src/components/Logo.tsx
3. The logo is already integrated into all navigation bars

The logo image you showed has:
- Blue and green "W" with people icons
- "WEBEARN" text with "WEB" in blue and "EARN" in green
- "REFER • BRING • EARN" tagline below
- Upward arrow symbolizing growth

Recommended image specifications:
- Format: PNG with transparent background
- Dimensions: 800x265 pixels (or similar 3:1 ratio)
- File size: Under 100KB for fast loading

Once you save the logo as public/logo.png, it will automatically appear throughout the site!
